\section*{About}

I'm a full stack engineer with 13 years of experience and a passion for
inclusive design. User needs, design systems, performance, and accessibility are
my jam.

I have led teams, and I don't mind getting my hands dirty across the whole
stack. I like the Lean Startup method, being an active participant in the design
and research process, and the Retrospective Prime Directive. I have a Bachelor's
degree in Engineering.
